# UI & Pacing Changes

1. Removed center-of-pitch announcement box at all times during the match.
2. Added small outcome/replay/next-ball strip directly above controls, never over the bowler-to-batter sightline.
3. Automatically advances after 8 s (10 s after overs), but Replay and Pause freeze the countdown.
4. Final innings screen appears after 8.5 s so players can replay the final delivery.
5. Removed public Phase 3 / Phase 4 development marketing badges. Quality notes remain in optional QA info page.
6. Introduced gentler 1.45 s run-up and eased fielders' rotation, without changing bowling speed, shots, scoring or game modes.
7. Source project is NOT the Y8 upload format until a validated `dist/` is created.
